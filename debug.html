This is an interactive problem.

Out of boredom, you decided to pass the time by playing a game of your own invention with the onboard supercomputer. The game works as follows: the supercomputer shows you a bipartite graph GGGG on nnnn vertices, and then chooses a secret pair of distinct edges e1e1e1e_1 and e2e2e2e_2 that are absent from it and have the following properties:

  • the graph G∪{e1}G∪{e1} is bipartite;
  • the graph G∪{e2}G∪{e2} is bipartite;
  • the graph G′=G∪{e1,e2}G′=G∪{e1,e2} is not bipartite.

Your task is to guess at least one of the secret edges. To do this, you may use two types of queries:

  • ? 1 kv1v2…vk (1≤k≤n, all vi are distinct): choose a set of k vertices and find out whether the subgraph of G′, consisting only of these vertices and the edges of G′ connecting them, is bipartite;
  • ? 2 uv (1≤u,v≤n, u≠v): find out whether G′ contains the edge (u,v).

It is guaranteed that graph GGG is such that the supercomputer can choose a pair of edges with the properties described above.

Since the manufacturer of supercomputers of your model has introduced new quotas on RAM usage, you may make no more than 333333 queries.

Note that the interactor is non-adaptive: the hidden edges e1e1e_1 and e2e2e_2 are fixed before the interaction begins and do not depend on your queries.

Interaction

For each test case, first read graph GGG in the format described above, after which you may make no more than 333333 queries of two types:

  • ? 1 kv1v2…vk (1≤k≤n, all vi are distinct). In response, you will receive 1 if the subgraph of G′, consisting only of the specified vertices and the edges of G′ connecting them, is bipartite, and 0 otherwise.
  • ? 2 uv (1≤u,v≤n, u≠v). In response, you will receive 1 if the edge (u,v) is present in graph G′, and 0 otherwise.

To report your answer, output one of the hidden edges in the format ! uvu  vu \; v. After that, immediately proceed to the next test case. Outputting the answer does not count as a query.

After printing each query do not forget to output the end of line and flush∗∗^{\text{∗}} the output. Otherwise, you will get Idleness limit exceeded verdict.

If, at any interaction step, you read −1−1-1 instead of valid data, your solution must exit immediately. This means that your solution will receive Wrong answer because of an invalid query or any other mistake. Failing to exit can result in an arbitrary verdict because your solution will continue to read from a closed stream.

Hack format

The first line contains one integer ttt (1≤t≤1041≤t≤1041 \le t \le 10^4) — the number of test cases.

The first line of each test case contains two integers nnn and mmm, the next mmm lines contain the edges of graph GGG in the format described above. Each of the next two lines contains two integers uuu and vvv — the edges e1e1e_1 and e2e2e_2. Graph GGG and edges e1e1e_1, e2e2e_2 must satisfy all the constraints and properties described above.
